# ScaleVexo CRM — user guide

For the people who use it every day. Each section is one job; the screen named
in bold is where you do it.

## Getting in

- You join through an **invitation link** an administrator sends you. Open it,
  choose a password of at least 12 characters, and you land on **Today**. A link
  works once and expires after 14 days. If it says "not valid", ask for a new one.
- Owners and administrators must set up **two-factor authentication** (the link
  is in the header). Until then, team management, receipts, rules and export
  stay locked — this is deliberate, not a fault.

## Your day — Today

- **Overdue**, **Due today**, **Blocked** and **Upcoming** list your tasks.
  Overdue stays separate so you can see how far behind you are.
- **Record outcome**: say what happened, then either schedule the next action or
  say why there is none. Every completed follow-up ends in one or the other.
- **Reschedule** needs a reason. The original deadline stays on record next to
  the new one; three reschedules in a week raise a reviewable item, not a penalty.
- **Alerts** say why they appeared. **Open the record** goes to the cause;
  **Acknowledge** means you have seen it; **Resolve** records what you did.

## Leads and Deals

- **New lead**: a name and one contact method are enough to start.
- **Open** a lead to:
  - **Activity** — log a call, email, meeting or LinkedIn touch and schedule its
    follow-up in the same step. Manual entries are always marked
    *self-reported*; only a provider-confirmed event is marked as verified.
    Made a mistake? **Correct** adds a linked correction; the original stays.
  - **Details** — fix the person's details and the lead's notes. Managers also
    see **possible duplicates** here and can merge after review.
  - **Status** — qualification. Qualifying asks for the need and the fit;
    nurture asks for a review date; disqualifying asks for a reason.
  - **Owner** — managers reassign, with a reason.
  - **Open a deal** — what is being sold, and its value if agreed. Leave the
    value blank if it is not agreed: it shows as *not yet known*, never as zero.
- **Deal pipeline**: move a deal with the stage menu (no dragging). A proposal
  needs a scope reference; lost needs a reason. **Edit** changes value, currency
  and expected close on an open deal.
- **Close as won** (sales managers and the owner) needs the accepted scope, the
  commercial reference and a delivery owner. It creates the client and the
  onboarding project together. It records a contract, not money.

## Clients and Projects

- **Clients** lists handovers waiting for delivery. **Accept handover**, or
  **Return to sales** saying exactly what is missing. Click a client's name for
  the full record: what was promised, how the sale went, delivery and support.
- **Projects**: start a milestone, add tasks to it, **Submit for review** with
  evidence a reviewer can check. A reviewer **accepts** it or **sends it back**
  saying what must change. A milestone waiting on another cannot be accepted
  unless a manager records an override with a reason.
- **Mark blocked** needs what is blocking it and who acts next; **Resume work**
  when it clears. **Cancel milestone** needs a reason and its impact.
- **Scope changes and defects** are recorded separately: a scope change is new
  work the client asked for; a defect is work already owed.

## Tickets

- **New ticket**: say whether the client asked or you found it, and the priority.
  *Critical* notifies the incident owner once rule A07 is switched on.
- Waiting on the customer and waiting on us are different states; each needs a
  reason, a next owner and a review time.
- **Resolve** needs a resolution note *and* the result of a closure test.
  Reopening sends it back to triage and keeps the earlier resolution.
- **Internal only** is the default for comments and is marked with a lock.

## Team

- The team list, and **Accountability**: specific records in specific states
  that need a conversation — a missed deadline, a deal with no next action.
  You see the ones about your work and can explain or dispute the facts. A
  manager closes each with a written decision. Nothing here is a score, and the
  system takes no action on its own.

## Reports (managers)

- Open pipeline, won contract value and cash received are three separate
  figures, per currency, never added together.
- Click any figure, or **Show the deals / receipts**, to see the records behind
  it. The count always matches the figure.
- **Cash receipts** (owner): record money against a bank or payment reference.
  Mistakes are corrected with an adjusting entry; the original is kept.

## Settings (administrators)

- **Team**: invite (you get a one-time link to send privately), suspend, reinstate.
  Suspension ends access on the next request and keeps everything the person did.
- **Rules and alerts**: rules start off. **Simulate** against a real record,
  then **Turn on**. **Pause** also stops work already queued. **Edit** saves a
  new version.
- **Working calendar**: working days, hours and holidays. Deadlines count only
  working time; changing the calendar does not move deadlines already set.
- **Export** (owner): a ZIP of every record with stable identifiers. Audited.
